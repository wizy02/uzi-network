# The free calendar that finally got me to switch

I was a die-hard Google Calendar user. Then Notion (the company) acquired Cron and rebuilt it as Notion Calendar. I tried it on a whim. Six months later, I haven't opened Google Calendar once.

## What changed

The "killer feature" of Notion Calendar isn't any single thing — it's the **sum** of small things:

- **Two-way Google sync** — edits in either app appear in the other within seconds
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — `N` for new event, `T` for today, `J/K` for next/prev day — match Gmail, Linear, and every other modern productivity app
- **Notion AI integration** — daily brief in the morning, meeting prep notes pulled from past meetings
- **Multi-calendar overlay** — see work, personal, and shared calendars in one view, color-coded
- **Free** — no tier gating the core features

The Google Calendar I'm replacing is essentially the same product it was in 2018. Notion Calendar is what Google Calendar should have evolved into.

## The honest complaints

**1. Slow initial sync.**
When I connected my 6 Google Calendars, the initial sync took ~20 minutes. After that, instant. But for the first hour, events lagged.

**2. No CalDAV.**
This means third-party calendar apps (Fantastical on iOS, Thunderbird) can't connect to Notion Calendar's events. You're locked into the Notion Calendar app. For me, fine — the iOS app is solid. But a real limitation.

**3. Mobile app.**
The macOS app is excellent. The iOS app is good. The Android app is "fine." None of them are best-in-class — Fantastical on iOS still wins the mobile crown.

## Who should switch

**Switch if:**
- You're already using Notion for docs, wikis, or projects
- You use multiple Google calendars and want better overlay UX
- You want free meeting prep / daily brief AI features
- Keyboard shortcuts matter to you

**Stay with Google Calendar if:**
- You use Fantastical, BusyCal, or another CalDAV app
- You're in Outlook / Microsoft 365 (Notion Calendar syncs with those but Microsoft users tend to prefer Outlook)
- You need the absolute simplest possible interface

## The free-vs-paid picture

Notion Calendar is genuinely free for everything you'd actually use. The Notion AI features are included if you have any Notion paid plan — but the calendar itself is free regardless.

Compare to:
- Fantastical: $5/mo (iOS/Mac), $10/yr (Android)
- Google Calendar: free but Microsoft 365 integration is paid
- Calendly for scheduling: $8-16/mo

For free, Notion Calendar is the best deal in this space.

## Verdict

Notion Calendar is the calendar app Google should have built. It's free, fast, integrated with the Notion ecosystem, and respects power-user keyboard shortcuts.

I'm not switching back.
