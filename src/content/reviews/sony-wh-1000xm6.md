# The headphones I keep coming back to

I owned the XM5. I bought the XM6 the week they launched. Here's what changed, what didn't, and whether you should upgrade.

## What's actually new

Sony positioned the XM6 as a refinement, not a revolution. After six weeks, that framing is correct:

- **Better ANC** — not by a lot, but noticeable on planes
- **Improved call quality** — the XM5 was bad on calls, the XM6 is actually usable
- **New hinge design** — folds flat and folds up, like the XM4
- **Touch controls feel snappier** — minor but appreciated

What's NOT new:
- Battery life (still excellent)
- App (same Sony Headphones Connect)
- Multipoint behavior (still good, still occasionally flaky on Windows)

## ANC: still the king

This is the headline feature and Sony still wins it. On a recent NYC → SFO flight, the XM6 killed engine drone completely and reduced cabin chatter to a whisper. The AirPods Max (still my favorite for Apple ecosystem users) wasn't close.

For working in cafes, on transit, in open offices — nothing else I've tested comes close.

## The honest complaints

**1. The price hike.**
The XM6 launched at $449. The XM5 launched at $399. In a market where competition is fiercer than in 2022, this is annoying.

**2. The bass.**
The XM6 has Sony's signature warm, bass-forward tuning. If you want analytical/neutral sound for mixing or audio work, look at the Sennheiser HD 660S2 or Audeze Maxwell instead.

**3. No lossless.**
LDAC is the ceiling. For lossless audio, you need wired headphones (or the AirPods Max with Apple Music).

## Should you upgrade from XM5?

Honestly? **Probably not.**

The improvements are real but incremental. Unless the XM6's better call quality matters to your work (it does for me), the XM5 is 95% as good for $100 less now.

## Should you buy XM6 vs AirPods Max?

If you're all-Apple: AirPods Max, especially the USB-C version.
If you split time between Apple and Windows/Android: Sony XM6, no contest.
If ANC is your #1 priority: Sony XM6.

## My final take

The XM6 is the best noise-cancelling headphone you can buy in 2026. The price is up, but only by $50 over the previous king. If you travel or work in noise, you will not regret this purchase.

Skip if you already own XM5. Buy if you don't.
