    <p>Most 10,000mAh banks are bulky. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com?tag=uzinetwork20-20" rel="sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Anker PowerCore 10K</a> is 0.6 inches thin — about the thickness of two phones stacked.     <h2>USB-C + USB-A is forward and backward compatible</h2>
    <p>The PowerCore 10K has both USB-C and USB-A output ports. USB-C for modern phones, USB-A for older cables, earbuds, smartwatches. You don't need to remember to bring the right cable.</p>

    <h2>What I didn't like</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Slow input recharging.</strong> The bank itself takes 4+ hours to fully recharge from a 10W wall charger. Use an 18W+ charger to cut that in half.</li>
      <li><strong>Not for laptops.</strong> 10,000mAh at 12W max output is fine for phones and tablets, but won't charge a MacBook at any meaningful rate. If you need laptop charging, get the Anker 737 (24K mAh, 140W).</li>
      <li><strong>No fast charging on output.</strong> Phones that support 20W+ fast charging will charge at standard 12W speed. Not slow, just not fast.</li>
