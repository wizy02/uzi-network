## The 7 Models (What Works in 2026)

### 1. Usage-Based Pricing ($0.01-$1 per task)
- Charge per API call, token, action
- Best for: high-volume, variable workloads
- Examples: voice agents, document processing
- **For us:** Could charge per affiliate link clicked (after the click)
- **Realistic monthly:** $0-200/mo with 100-1K users

### 2. Agent-Based Pricing ($3,000-$20,000/month fixed)
- Best for: FTE replacement (customer service, SDR)
- **For us:** Not viable — too high-touch sales, requires enterprise trust
- Skip.

### 3. Outcome-Based Pricing (percentage of value)
- Charge % of revenue generated
- Best for: agents that close deals, drive revenue
- **For us:** Perfect fit for affiliate content agent
- Could charge 10-20% of affiliate revenue for any user who uses our content
- **Realistic monthly:** $0-500/mo (depends on user base)

### 4. Workflow-Based Pricing ($50-$2,000 per workflow)
- Charge per completed automated workflow
- **For us:** We already do this — could sell "done-for-you" content packages
- **Realistic monthly:** $100-1,000/mo (5-10 packages at $50-200 each)

### 5. Subscription ($5-500/month)
- Recurring monthly fee for ongoing access
- **For us:** Newsletter premium tier ($9-29/mo for exclusive drops)
- **Realistic monthly:** $90-1,000/mo at 10-50 subscribers

### 6. Freemium (free tier + premium)
- Free = lead magnet, paid = power features
- **For us:** Free PDF + free site, paid = "Uzi Network Pro" with exclusive AI tool discount codes
- **Realistic monthly:** $200-1,000/mo (20-50 paid × $10-20)

### 7. Sponsored Content (single highest $ per deal)
- $200-2,000 per newsletter slot
- $500-5,000 per YouTube integration
- **For us:** HIGHEST ROI per effort
- **Realistic monthly:** $200-1,500/mo with 1-3 sponsors
- **Action:** Pitch Anthropic, Notion, ElevenLabs, Jasper for sponsored slots
