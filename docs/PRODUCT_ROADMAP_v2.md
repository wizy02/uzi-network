## The Money Tag + Adsterra Layer

**Where they fit:**

| Network | Use case | Payment model |
|---|---|---|
| **Money Tag** | Affiliate aggregator — offers from many networks in one link | CPA, varies by offer |
| **Adsterra** | Display ads alternative to Ezoic | CPM, popunder, social bar |
| **Ezoic** (existing) | Primary display ad network | CPM, AI-optimized |
| **Mediavine** (target) | Higher-paying display ad network | CPM, premium |

**Strategy:**

1. **Ezoic = primary display** (already set up)
2. **Amazon = primary affiliate** (EPC $0.20–$0.50)
3. **Brand direct = secondary affiliate** (EPC $0.50–$2.00)
4. **Awin/Impact = tertiary affiliate** (EPC varies, but unlocks EU markets)
5. **Money Tag = arbitrage** (push lower-quality clicks to high-CPA offers; reserve for "bonus" content)
6. **Adsterra = fallback** (if Ezoic doesn't pay out at scale, switch)

**Important:** Money Tag and Adsterra should NOT replace Amazon. They supplement. Amazon wins for tech, period.

## Money Tag Integration

**What it is:** A link monetization service. You wrap your affiliate links in their redirector. They add their own affiliate IDs and split the revenue.

**How we use it:**

1. Sign up at https://www.moneytag.com/
2. Get your monetized link pattern: `https://uzi-network.moneytag.link/click?url=ENCODED`
3. Wrap every Amazon + brand direct affiliate link
4. Track earnings in MoneyTag dashboard
5. Use the multi-offer rotator: each click can go to one of several offers, weighted by EPC

**Pros:** Adds 10–30% to affiliate revenue on average. The split is usually 70/30 in your favor.

**Cons:** Adds a redirect (slight speed cost). Some affiliate programs don't allow link wrapping (check ToS).

**Setup time:** 1 hour. Worth it.
