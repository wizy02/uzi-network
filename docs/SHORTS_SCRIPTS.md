## Production Pipeline

For each Short, we need:
1. **Voiceover** (60s of speech) — generated via TTS, can swap to human voice later
2. **Stock footage** (3–5 clips from Pexels/Pixabay)
3. **Text overlays** (3–5 lines, our brand colors: black + #uzi-500)
4. **Background music** (free from Pixabay Music, low volume)
5. **End card** with site URL + "Full review on uzinetwork.store"

**Tools (all free):**
- **CapCut** (free desktop editor, auto-captions)
- **Pexels/Pixabay** for footage
- **ElevenLabs free tier** for voiceover (10,000 chars/month, enough for ~30 Shorts)
- **Canva free tier** for text overlays and end cards

**Time per Short**: 20–30 min in batch mode. 30 Shorts = 10–15 hours of work.

**I generate the scripts (done). Operator does the editing or hires a $50/month VA to do it.**
